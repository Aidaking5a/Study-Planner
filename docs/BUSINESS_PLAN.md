# Kloer Business Plan

## One-line business

Kloer helps Luxembourg students turn multilingual school material into clear explanations, quizzes, flashcards, and study plans.

## Brand

Name: Kloer

Meaning: "clear" in Luxembourgish.

Tagline: Study clearly, in every language.

## Target customer

Start with Luxembourg secondary students and their parents. The first buyer is likely a parent or motivated student preparing for tests. The first user is the student.

Beachhead segments:

- Upper-secondary students with STEM-heavy subjects.
- Students moving between German, French, English, and Luxembourgish contexts.
- Parents who already pay for tutoring or extra school support.
- Tutors who want faster prep material for their students.

## Problem

Students do not only struggle with the topic. They often struggle with the language around the topic. A concept can be taught in German, tested in French, discussed in Luxembourgish, and searched online in English.

Kloer starts by making the material clear in the student's chosen language, then converts it into active recall.

## MVP

The first product should do five things well:

1. Accept pasted notes or text uploads.
2. Accept phone photos of notes and transcribe them locally.
3. Explain the material at the student's level.
4. Generate flashcards and quiz questions.
5. Create a concrete study plan before a test.
6. Create topic-specific student PowerPoints from the same notes.

PDF upload, school integrations, parent dashboards, and community features come later.

## Pricing

Free beta:

- Local/template agents.
- Limited sessions.
- Used for validation and interviews.

Paid v1:

- Student Plus: EUR 9.99/month.
- Family: EUR 19.99/month for up to 3 students.
- Exam Pack: EUR 19-49 one-time, focused on a subject or test period.
- Tutor plan: EUR 199-499/month once tutor workflows are validated.

## Year-one revenue path

Goal: roughly EUR 100k annualized revenue.

Possible mix:

- 600 students at EUR 9.99/month.
- 150 family plans at EUR 19.99/month.
- 10 tutor or learning-center accounts at EUR 249/month.
- Seasonal exam packs to smooth acquisition.

## Go-to-market

Phase 1: founder-led validation

- Interview 50 students, 20 parents, and 5 tutors.
- Build in public on TikTok, Instagram, and LinkedIn.
- Ask every beta user what test they are preparing for.

Phase 2: exam period wedge

- Launch subject-specific exam packs.
- Publish short multilingual explainers.
- Use referral codes for friend groups and classes.

Phase 3: tutor partnerships

- Give tutors a prep workspace.
- Let tutors invite students.
- Charge the tutor only after they use it repeatedly.

Phase 4: expansion

- Border regions in Belgium, France, and Germany.
- University students studying abroad.
- B2B education pilots after compliance hardening.

## Moat

Generic AI study apps can copy features. Kloer's moat should be distribution and context:

- Luxembourg-first multilingual UX.
- Curriculum-aware study packs.
- Trust with parents and tutors.
- Privacy-first EU posture.
- Local student community.
- Data on which concepts break across languages.

## Compliance guardrails

Start as self-study support. Avoid official decisions:

- no grading;
- no admissions;
- no ranking;
- no school placement;
- no automated teacher evaluation.

For minors:

- collect the minimum data;
- delete uploads by default;
- require parental authorization where needed;
- keep a plain-language privacy notice;
- do not sell student data.

## 30-day plan

Week 1:

- Use this MVP with 5 friendly users.
- Collect exact wording of their study pain.
- Replace generic copy with student language.

Week 2:

- Interview 20 more students.
- Add the most-requested subject flow.
- Publish 5 short demo videos.

Week 3:

- Run private beta with 30 students.
- Add basic analytics events.
- Ask for first testimonials.

Week 4:

- Test a EUR 9.99/month pre-order or EUR 19 exam pack.
- Contact 5 tutors.
- Decide whether to add LLM-backed generation.

## Funding path

Do not fundraise before you have usage. First prove:

- students return before tests;
- parents understand the value;
- tutors can save time;
- users will pay for better output.

After that, prepare for local startup support and accelerators with:

- active users;
- retention;
- testimonials;
- first revenue;
- clear AI and data-protection guardrails.
